1. Who we are

This website is operated by ECO-Fridge Ltd, 18 Top Angel, Buckingham Industrial Park, Buckingham, Buckinghamshire MK18 1TH, United Kingdom ("ECO-Fridge", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Questions about this policy or your information: email sales@eco-fridge.co.uk, call 01280 811411 or write to us at the address above.

2. Information we collect

  • Orders and quotes – your name, company name, billing and delivery addresses, email address, telephone number, the products you order and any delivery instructions.
  • Payments – card payments are processed securely by our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card number; we receive confirmation that the payment was made. For bank transfers we receive the payer details shown on the transfer.
  • Your account – if you create an account: your login details, saved addresses and order history.
  • Enquiries – the information you give us when you fill in a contact form, email or call us, and our replies.
  • Website use – technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit and how you arrived at our site, collected through server logs and cookies (see Cookies).

We do not ask for special category information (such as health data), and our website is not intended for children.

3. How we use your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

4. Who we share it with

We only share your information where it is needed for the purposes above, with:

  • delivery and courier companies, to deliver your order;
  • our payment provider and bank, to take and confirm payments;
  • refrigeration engineers and service partners, where you need warranty or after-sales support;
  • providers of our website hosting, email and IT services;
  • Google, for website analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies;
  • our accountants and professional advisers, and HMRC or other authorities where the law requires it.

These organisations may only use your information to provide their services to us, or as the law allows.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers (for example email and analytics providers) may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we make sure the transfer is protected – for example by a UK adequacy decision or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

  • Orders, invoices and payment records – six years after the end of the financial year in which the order was placed, as required for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Enquiries and quotes that do not lead to an order – up to two years.
  • Your account – until you ask us to close it (order records are then kept as above).
  • Marketing preferences – until you unsubscribe; we keep a record of the unsubscribe so we don't contact you again.
  • Analytics data – for the period set in our analytics account (no more than 14 months).

7. Security

Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, payments are handled by our payment provider, and access to customer information is limited to staff and partners who need it. No system is completely secure, but we take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of your information based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • receive information you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use these rights, contact us using the details in Contact us. We will reply within one month. Some rights are limited by law – for example we must keep invoices for tax purposes. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Some are essential for the website to work; others help us understand how the site is used. We only set non-essential cookies if you agree to them.

Our Cookie Policy lists every cookie in more detail and explains how to control them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and remove your consent to analytics cookies at any time. If you block essential cookies, the basket and checkout will not work. Pages that show third-party content (for example embedded YouTube videos) may set cookies controlled by that provider, under its own privacy policy.

10. Links, children and changes

Our website links to other websites, such as social networks. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies. Our website and services are aimed at businesses and are not intended for children.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
