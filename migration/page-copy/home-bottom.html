Find the right equipment

  • Multideck, grab and go and patisserie displays sell chilled food front of house.
  • Glass door drinks fridges and back bar chillers keep bottles cold and visible for less energy than open shelves.
  • Display freezers and chest freezers sell ice cream and frozen lines.

  • Heated displays keep pastries and hot snacks at serving temperature.

Check before you buy

  • Space – measure width, depth and height, allow ventilation space around the cabinet and check the delivery route.
  • Temperature – chilled cabinets usually run from −1 to +7 °C and freezers at −18 °C or colder; meat fridges run colder than standard fridges.
  • Room conditions – most display cabinets are designed for rooms up to 25 °C; choose kitchen-rated models next to ovens and grills.
  • Running costs – glass doors, night blinds and LED lighting keep energy bills down.
