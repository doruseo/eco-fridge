1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They let the site remember things between pages and visits – for example what is in your basket, or that you are signed in. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way, and this policy covers them too.

Some cookies are set by us (first-party cookies). Others are set by services we use or content we show from other websites, such as Google (third-party cookies). Some last only until you close your browser (session cookies); others stay for a set period (persistent cookies).

2. How we use cookies

  • Strictly necessary – needed for the website to work: the basket, checkout, your account and security. They cannot be switched off in our systems, and the law does not require consent for them.
  • Analytics – Google Analytics shows us how many people visit, which pages they use and how they found us, so we can improve the site. These cookies are only set if you agree to them.
  • Third-party content – maps and videos from Google Maps and YouTube may set their own cookies when they load (see section 4).

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies to show you adverts on other websites, and we do not sell information collected through cookies.

3. The cookies we use

Strictly necessary

When you pay by card, our payment provider may set its own strictly necessary cookies on its secure payment pages to process the payment and prevent fraud.

Analytics (only with your consent)

Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay and your approximate location (derived from your IP address, which is not stored in full). Google processes this information on our behalf; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Third-party content

  • Google Maps – the map on our Contact Us page is provided by Google and may set cookies such as NID to remember your preferences and secure the service.
  • YouTube – product and guide videos on some pages are embedded from YouTube, which may set cookies such as YSC, VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE and PREF when a video loads or is played.
  • Social links – links to our social media pages do not set cookies until you visit those sites.

These cookies are controlled by the third parties, under their own policies: Google and YouTube privacy policy.

5. Your choices

We ask for your consent before setting analytics cookies, and you can change your mind at any time by clearing the cookies for our site in your browser – we will then ask again on your next visit. You can also stop Google Analytics on every website with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

If you refuse or delete cookies, the website still works, except that strictly necessary cookies are needed for the basket, checkout and your account.

6. Browser settings

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies. Find out how in your browser's help pages:

  • Google Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari (Mac) and Safari (iPhone and iPad)

For general information about cookies, see the ICO's guide at ico.org.uk.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when we change the cookies we use. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top. How we handle personal information more generally is explained in our Privacy Policy.
