Upright Freezer Maintenance

Commercial Freezer Maintenance is important to your business, saving you time, energy and money. Commercial freezers are an essential part of most businesses, allowing you to buy food in bulk and save plenty of time and money. Occasionally the display freezer starts to work poorly or even stops altogether, which can cause serious problems. Whether you have a chest freezer or an upright display freezer, it is worth understanding proper commercial freezer maintenance so that you can keep it running efficiently and smoothly for the time being.

Auto-defrost option

Some upright freezers (such as the meat freezer) have an automatic defrost cycle that usually runs twice a day and removes ice from the coils. The resulting water then drains into a pan at the base of the cabinet. Normally, the water should evaporate by itself, so you should not need to empty the pan. However, you should check the pan and clean it with washing-up liquid to make sure it is not overflowing, becoming smelly or growing mould. You also need to clean the dust from the condenser coils and cooling fan at least once a year. If you allow a build-up to occur, your commercial upright freezer will have to work harder and use more energy to stay cold. Unplug the freezer, then use a brush and vacuum to remove the dirt. It is best not to use compressed air to blow out the dirt, as this will drive the dust further into the cabinet, causing it to stick and settle in the fan.

Manual defrost

Some upright display freezers do not have an automatic defrost feature and so require manual defrosting. When ice has built up on the walls, you will need to carry out the same defrosting routine. First, remove all food from the display freezer, then switch it off. Wait for the ice to melt on its own, then wipe it away. Finally, you can return the food once the temperature has fallen to 0˚F.
