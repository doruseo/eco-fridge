Cleaning the Storage Freezer

A dirty, iced-over freezer can drastically reduce efficiency, push up your energy bill and spoil your stored food. Unpleasant smells and tastes can affect your products. You can avoid this by giving your unit a thorough clean. Ideally, you should clean your freezer at least twice a year.    

1. Wipe the ice down with a warm cloth.

You need to melt any ice that may have built up inside your storage freezer. Start by using a cloth dipped in hot, soapy water to wipe it down. Once the edges begin to melt, the pieces of ice should break away. When they come away, put them in the sink. Use a damp cloth to wipe down the walls and floor of the freezer to remove any remaining ice.  

2. Clean the storage freezer with a sponge.

Use a microfibre cloth dipped in your cleaning solution to wipe down the door, compartments and floor of your freezer. You may need to repeat this step to remove any stubborn stains. Remember to clean the gasket too. Using an old toothbrush and the cleaning solution, scrub off any dirt and wipe away the residue with a sponge.  

3. Dry your commercial freezer.

Before you put anything back, dry the storage freezer with a microfibre cloth. This will remove any remaining moisture inside. That way, you prevent any further ice build-up once you close the door and the temperature drops.  

4. Wash the shelves and drawers.

You should clean the drawers and shelves before returning them to the storage freezer. Use warm, soapy water to clean them and dry them with a soft cloth. Put them back in the freezer and close the door.  

5. Return the food to the commercial freezer.

First, wait for the temperature to drop. Then you can return any products you took out, and you are all done.  

6. Tips for correct cleaning

– Avoid scraping the ice with a knife, as you may damage the walls of the commercial freezer. – Do not pour boiling water directly onto the walls, as this may damage them. Be sure to wipe up any excess water from the floor afterwards to prevent slips and falls. – If you do not have time to clean your storage freezer, there are other options. Most modern models have a self-defrosting feature, which makes cleaning them quicker and easier. – If you are not ready for a new piece of equipment, you can call in a professional cleaner.
