What is a glass front fridge?

A glass front fridge is a refrigerator with a glass front that allows customers to see what is inside. Depending on how you use it, this can be a cake fridge, a deli fridge or a countertop fridge. A glass front fridge can also have doors, in which case it is known as a glass door fridge.  

Where can I use a glass front fridge?

Glass front fridges are popular in commercial settings, such as restaurants, takeaways, cake shops, cafes and bars.  

What products can I chill in a glass front fridge?

Glass front fridges are suitable for chilling all types of food, such as meat and dairy products, and are also perfect for drinks like wine and beer.  

How often should I clean a glass front fridge?

Glass front fridges should be cleaned every few weeks using a solution of vinegar and water. The vinegar helps remove any smudges or fingerprints on the glass. To clean your glass front fridge, mix a solution of one part vinegar to three parts water. Use a soft cloth to wipe down the inside and outside of the fridge, including the door gasket. Rinse with clean water and dry with a clean towel. Repeat this every few weeks to keep your fridge looking its best.  

What temperature range should a glass front fridge work in?

A glass front fridge should operate in a temperature range of 0–10 degrees Celsius, or 32–50 degrees Fahrenheit.
