What is a Multideck Fridge?

The term "multideck" is a fairly broad one that covers any multi-tiered display refrigeration. Display chiller cabinets are the most popular type of glass-front fridge for most shops. Featuring several shelves in the tiered display fridge means that your products are visible, attractive and stored in ideal conditions. By creating an attractive display that appeals to customers, every available product is presented appealingly to encourage an enjoyable shopping experience and increase sales. Mostly found in superstores and markets that want to maximise the presentation of chilled goods, these open-front fridge displays are perfect for encouraging "grab and go" purchases. Configurations are available to hold fresh meats, fruit and vegetables, dairy products, and general-purpose goods, meaning that this versatile equipment can be tailored to meet all display needs. Our open fridges for shops are integral or plug-in. That means all parts are housed inside the equipment, with no external components other than the electrical supply. The condensing unit/motor is usually located beneath the bottom shelf.  

Advantages of plug-in/integral units:

- Plug-in or integral pieces of equipment are more affordable to buy, keeping initial costs low. - As running noises stay within the premises, there is little risk of complaints from neighbours. - No planning permissions are required for integral displays, a consent that is sometimes necessary for remote alternatives. - In most cases, plug-in models are reliable when serviced and maintained properly.  

Sizes:

The size of the commercial refrigeration equipment you choose is important. After all, you need something that will fit into the space available without obstructing or overcrowding walkways. The smallest multideck is no more than 600mm long, and the largest model in our range is 2500mm long. An immediate indication of the size is the multideck's capacity. Clearly, the larger the dimensions, the more space will be available for stocking products. The storage capacity will also depend on the number and configuration of shelves that each unit holds.  

Temperature and Contents:

The temperature you need will be determined by the products you wish to display. Generally, the temperature will fall anywhere between 0˚C and 10˚C, providing ideal conditions for most refrigerated produce. For example, dairy, soft drinks, cooked meats, sandwiches, and fruit and vegetables. If you are looking to feature a cut of fresh meat in a tiered display, you will need a lower temperature range to maintain food safety, typically in the region of 0°C to 2°C. If you need to store frozen food, then you should check the Display Freezer category. Freezers are available with a temperature range from -18°C to -22°C, suitable for all frozen produce.
