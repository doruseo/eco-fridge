Storage Fridge

Depending on the type of food, you will need to store it in the commercial fridge or freezer, or in containers kept in cupboards or on shelves. When storing food, you need to chill it to ensure it remains safe to eat or cook. Some goods should stay in the storage fridge to help prevent micro-organisms from developing on them. For example, food with a 'use by' date, cooked dishes and ready-to-eat food such as desserts and cooked meats. To begin with, let's answer a few basic questions.  

What is a commercial refrigerator?

A commercial refrigerator is a cooling appliance that keeps things cold. It is also called a commercial fridge. It typically holds the interior temperature at 4-5 degrees Celsius. You can store food and drinks in it to keep them cold for a longer period. A refrigerator has a heat pump. It removes the warm interior air and transfers it outside. The heat pump is usually powered by an electric motor. Some refrigerators don't use electricity. These are filled with ice to provide the low temperature. The ice keeps goods cold by absorbing their energy until the ice melts. These coolers are commonly used on picnics. They are sometimes called cool boxes. Refrigerators of this kind were used before electricity became widely available.  

What is a commercial fridge?

A commercial fridge is a storage chiller or display fridge used in business environments. For instance, in restaurants, hotels, bars, catering businesses and shops. The commercial refrigeration unit is designed to provide more space for holding larger quantities of food. This is the primary reason for choosing commercial fridges in the food industry.  

How many years do commercial refrigerators last?

Answer: about 10 years With correct, regular maintenance, commercial fridges can have a life expectancy of up to 10 years. When these commercial refrigeration units are not properly cared for, they may develop premature mechanical faults and will require expensive repairs.  

What is the difference between a commercial fridge and a domestic refrigerator?

Commercial fridges are designed for far more frequent use. A domestic fridge may only be opened a dozen times a day, whereas a commercial fridge could be opened continually over the course of a few hours.  

Can you use a commercial fridge at home?

A commercial fridge is a popular addition to many homes. Depending on the size and layout of your home, you may want to consider either a standard commercial refrigerator alongside a separate commercial freezer, or a dual-temperature unit that combines them like a typical domestic model.

Ensure your storage fridge is sufficiently cold

You need to ensure your commercial refrigerator is cold enough, or food poisoning bacteria will still be able to grow. Your commercial fridge should be somewhere between 0ºC and 5ºC. If you're not sure how the temperature setting or dial works on your refrigerator, you can use a fridge thermometer to check it is at the correct temperature.

What do you need to know when storing food in the commercial refrigerator?

- keep the chiller door shut as much as possible - make sure that goods have cooled down before you put them in the fridge - turn the temperature down to help keep it cold enough when the chiller is full - keep the food in the storage fridge  

Help prevent microorganisms from developing:

- when the label says 'keep refrigerated', make sure you do keep the food in the fridge. If the food isn't marked with any storage instructions and it's a type of food that goes off quickly, you should place it in the chiller and eat it within two days. - some containers and jars should be kept in the refrigerator once they've been opened. - when you're preparing food, keep it out of the storage fridge for the shortest time possible, especially when the weather or the room is warm. - if you have made some food, such as a sandwich, and you're not going to eat it straight away, keep it in the fridge until you're ready to eat it. - if you're hosting a gathering or preparing a buffet, leave the food in the storage refrigerator until people are ready to eat. Moreover, you shouldn't keep food out of the storage chiller for more than 3 hours. - cool leftovers as quickly as possible (ideally within one to two hours) and then store them in the refrigerator. Eat any leftovers within two days, except for cooked rice, which you should eat within one day to help avoid food poisoning.  

Storing meat

It's important to store meat safely to prevent bacteria from spreading and to avoid food poisoning. So you should: - store raw meat and poultry in clean, sealed containers on the bottom shelf of the storage fridge, so they can't touch or drip onto other food. - follow any storage instructions on the label and don't eat meat after its use-by date. - when you have cooked meat and you're not going to eat it straight away, cool it as quickly as possible and then put it in the chiller. - store raw meat separately from cooked meat.  

Storing dry food in containers

Many types of goods shouldn't be kept in the chiller in order to keep them safe to eat. For example, dry foods (rice, pasta and flour), drinks, tinned foods, and unopened jars. Even so, it's important to take care of how you store them. To store dry food safely: - keep food in sealed bags or containers. This helps keep food fresh and stops anything from falling into the goods accidentally. - don't store food or drinks near cleaning products or other chemicals. - don't use old food containers to store household chemicals. And don't store food in containers that have been used for other purposes. - only reuse whole plastic water bottles that you can clean. - don't store food on the floor, since this can attract mice, ants and other pests. - keep the storage area dry and not too warm.  

Tin cans

When you open a can of food and you're not going to use all of it straight away, empty the goods into a bowl or another container and put it in the commercial fridge. Don't store food in an opened tin can, or reuse empty cans to prepare or store food. This is because, once a can has been opened and the product is exposed to the air, the tin from the can may transfer more quickly to the contents. This advice doesn't apply to food sold in cans that have resealable tops (such as golden syrup and cocoa), because these types of products don't react with the can.  

Covering food with aluminium foil

Aluminium cling film is useful for protecting food but, like many things, it should be used correctly. Not every kind of cling film is suitable for use with all goods. Aluminium can affect the taste of highly acidic foods such as: - soft fruit - cabbage - rhubarb - tomatoes
