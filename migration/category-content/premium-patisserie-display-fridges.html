Features of Premium Patisserie Display fridges

There are numerous features which are advantageous when choosing a patisserie display fridge.  
  • Removable bottom plates can provide easier cleaning access, ensuring that hygiene doesn't have to be hard work.
  • Auto defrost is another feature that proves useful, minimising the amount of elbow grease required to maintain equipment. Using electric elements to gently heat the evaporator, any ice build-up is melted and the water evaporates. Off-cycle defrost functions are also available that remove any ice build-up from the evaporator by temporarily pausing the refrigeration cycle.
  • Glass side panels can increase potential presentation when your patisserie display chiller is isolated, however, they are unnecessary if the unit is adjacent to other equipment.
  • Stainless steel finishes are more suitable than standard white or other colour options that may not match existing equipment or complement the style and decor of the premises.
  • Lighting is a great way to illuminate contents and present the stock in the most attractive manner. Our patisserie display fridges have a top-mounted premium LED light.
  • When shelves can be tilted, shelf risers are perfect for stopping produce from falling off onto the shop floor.
  • Castors are a great way to ensure easy movement and positioning of equipment, whether during initial installation or during routine cleaning, service and maintenance. The castors are an optional extra at an additional charge, so you need to tick this option.
  • Night blinds are soft curtains that can be drawn over the open-front display during out-of-hours periods. Although not a standard feature, they are a great way to increase energy efficiency.
