Storing Meat in the Upright Gastronorm Freezer

Meat stored in the storage freezer should be kept at 0°F (−18°C). Freezer storage times for cooked meats are shorter than for raw meat because of what is known as flavour transfer. Certain flavours oxidise in the storage freezer after a period of time. Once frozen meat has thawed, refreezing is not recommended unless it is cooked first, for several reasons: The quality suffers each time frozen meat is thawed and refrozen. Freezing creates ice crystals within the structure of the meat (as meat contains a high proportion of water). These ice crystals break down the fibres, which makes the meat lose moisture when thawed. If this is repeated, the texture of the meat will become very dry. Never refreeze meat that has been thawed and held at room temperature.  

How to defrost meat

The best way to defrost meat is to store it in a commercial fridge. Bear in mind that this is the slowest defrosting method. Small frozen items may thaw overnight in the commercial refrigerator, while larger items will take considerably longer. In a microwave on the defrost setting: plan to cook the food as soon as it has defrosted in the microwave, since some areas of the food may have started to cook during the defrost cycle.    

How long can you store meat in the Storage Freezer?

- Cooked meat: 2 to 3 months - Raw minced meat: 3 to 4 months - Cooked poultry: 4 months - Raw poultry portions: 9 months - Whole raw poultry: 12 months - Raw joints: 4 months to 1 year - Raw steaks or chops: 4 months to 1 year   Wrap meat in airtight cling film or freezer paper if you need to keep it for longer than a few months. The same applies to fish. Tasty and nutritious, meat and fish are a key part of most families' meals. The main drawback is that they will go off in under 7 days. There is a way to extend this, however. If you freeze your meat or fish in a commercial meat freezer, it can last much longer. Remarkably, frozen meat or fish kept at a temperature of 0°F (−18°C) or lower will be safe to eat for several months. Micro-organisms such as bacteria, yeast and mould cannot grow at this temperature. The longer you leave it in the meat freezer, the lower the quality of the meat. Meat can become freezer burnt, which means the surface dries out. Signs of freezer burn include dryness, white patches of ice crystals and discolouration.
