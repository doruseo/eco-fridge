Final considerations

One of the biggest mistakes people make is overfilling the storage chiller. When ingredients obstruct the airflow, it makes the upright fridge work harder and leaves certain areas of the meat chiller warmer than others. By taking a proactive approach and placing goods inside the meat fridge in a sensible way, you can maintain proper airflow and keep an even temperature. Do not place foods tight against the lower back or sides of the upright chiller, and aim to keep all the products separated by around half an inch. This ensures air can circulate between the spaces inside the meat chiller, and food will be far less likely to spoil. By mastering how to store food in the right areas of the storage fridge, it is possible to preserve nutrients and ensure that foods do not go off. It is also crucial to store food properly to reduce the chance of cross-contamination between items. Odours are one of the biggest problems in a commercial fridge. In addition, condensation and mould may spread if ingredients are not stored properly in the meat fridge. Keeping goods at a temperature below 40 Fahrenheit can restrict the growth of micro-organisms and help your food stay safe for longer.
