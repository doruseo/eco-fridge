Features of the Premium Upright Cake Display Fridge

There are many useful features to consider when choosing an upright cake display fridge.  
  • Removable base plates make cleaning easier, so hygiene doesn't have to be hard work.
  • Auto defrost is another handy feature that reduces maintenance: electric elements gently heat the evaporator, melting any ice build-up so the water evaporates. Off-cycle defrost is also available, clearing ice from the evaporator by briefly pausing the refrigeration cycle.
  • Glass side panels improve presentation when your patisserie display chiller stands alone, though they're less relevant when the unit sits beside other equipment.
  • Stainless steel finishes are often more suitable than standard white or other colours that may not match your existing equipment or complement the style and decor of your premises.
  • Lighting is a great way to illuminate the contents and present stock at its most attractive. Our premium upright cake display fridge has a top-mounted premium LED light.
  • Where shelves can be tilted, shelf risers are perfect for stopping produce from sliding off onto the floor.
  • Castors make it easy to move and position equipment, whether when first installing it or during routine cleaning and maintenance. This premium upright cake display fridge comes with four castors, so it's easy to transport, position and clean around.
