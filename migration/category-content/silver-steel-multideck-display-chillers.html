Standard multideck display chiller

An attractive multideck display chiller is a must for any hypermarket, superstore or supermarket looking to increase sales. Multidecks are a great way to present a wide range of products in perfectly chilled conditions. There are a number of things to consider before investing in a multideck display.  

What is a Multideck display chiller ?

The term "multideck" is a fairly broad one that covers any multi-tiered display refrigeration used for self-service. Typically open-fronted, display fridge cabinets are the most popular form of display cabinet for the majority of shops. Featuring multiple shelves, the tiered display chiller keeps your products visible, well presented and stored in optimum conditions. By creating an attractive display that appeals to customers, all available products are shown to best effect, encouraging a relaxed shopping experience and an increase in sales. Found mainly in superstores, shops and supermarkets that want to maximise the presentation of chilled goods, these multi-level fridge displays are ideal for encouraging impulse "grab and go" purchases. Configurations are available to hold fresh meat, fruit and vegetables, dairy products and general goods, meaning that this versatile equipment can be tailored to meet all display needs. Our multideck display chiller is integral, or plug-in. That means all components are housed within the equipment, with no external elements other than the electrical supply. The condensing unit and motor are typically located beneath the base shelf. Advantages of plug-in / integral units:
  • Plug-in or integral equipment is less expensive to buy, keeping initial costs low.
  • As the running noise stays within the premises, there is little risk of complaints from neighbours.
  • No planning permission is required for integral displays, a consent that is sometimes necessary for remote alternatives.
  • In most cases, plug-in models are generally reliable when serviced and maintained properly.

  1. Shopfitting Considerations
It is recommended that consideration be given to the environmental conditions that exist, or could exist, in store. The following should be noted:
  • Manufacturer's guidelines (instruction booklet) for the Multideck display chiller model type:
    • Temperature levels
    • Humidity levels
    • Minimum air gap required around the cabinet
  • An adequate escape route for the hot air generated by the condenser unit:
    • Suitable ventilation
  • Preventative maintenance:
    • Regular cleaning of the condenser unit
  Adequate Ventilation The heat generated by the Multideck display chiller must be able to escape away from the cabinet. A cabinet can produce a discharge temperature of over 50˚C when there is insufficient provision for air gaps around it, which can lead to poor performance and eventual breakdown. To prevent this, the manufacturer's guidelines specific to the cabinet should be followed, and where possible, it is recommended that the minimum recommended air gap be exceeded.   It is recommended that provision be made for an open section at the top of all multideck cabinets to allow the heat generated to escape away from the cabinet. Ideally, the heat is removed from the site by a ventilation system, to prevent any build-up around the cabinet.   Where this is not possible, alternative measures should be considered. For example, ceiling tiles that are out of view of customers could be removed, or grilles fitted, to allow the heat to dissipate as far away from the cabinet as possible. It is recommended that as much ventilation as possible be provided around the cabinet to prevent any build-up of heat.   Preventative Maintenance It is recommended that the area around the cabinet be kept clear and clean at all times. The condensing unit should be kept free of dust and other deposits by regular cleaning. If the condensing unit is not kept clean, performance will be adversely affected.  
  1. External Influences on Performance
To ensure a high level of performance is maintained, it is recommended that the following be noted:
  • Drawing plans:
    • The intended position of the Multideck display chiller – is the display to face a window or door?
    • Look for windows, doors, air conditioning units and extraction fans in close proximity
    • Low ceilings
  The Effect of Windows and Doors It is recommended that the multideck display fridges be positioned away from direct sunlight. Glass concentrates the sun's rays and creates a high level of heat. Cabinets positioned directly in the path of the sun's rays will have to work harder to maintain the required temperature.   Air Flow It is also recommended that the multideck display fridge be positioned away from strong passing air flow caused by open external doors or windows, because such air flow can cause large fluctuations in temperature. Similarly, air conditioning units, ceiling or floor fans, other refrigeration equipment and any other large electrical equipment can create air flow that has a detrimental effect on the performance of the cabinet.   Low Ceiling As heat rises, it builds up at ceiling level. If the ceiling is low, the hot air will remain at a low level and can migrate back into the cabinet, adversely affecting performance. It is recommended that measures be taken to dissipate this heat.
