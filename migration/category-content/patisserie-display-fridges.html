Features of patisserie display fridges

There are numerous features that are advantageous when choosing a patisserie display fridge.  
  • Removable bottom plates provide easier cleaning access, ensuring that hygiene doesn't have to be hard work.
  • Glass side panels can improve presentation when your patisserie display chiller stands on its own, though they serve little purpose if the unit sits next to other equipment.
  • Coated steel finishes are more suitable than standard white or other colour options that may not match existing equipment or complement the style and décor of the premises.
  • Lighting is a great way to illuminate the contents and present the stock as attractively as possible. Our patisserie display fridges have a top-mounted LED light.
  • When shelves can be tilted, shelf risers are perfect for stopping produce from falling onto the shop floor.
  • Castors are a great way to make it easy to move and position the equipment, whether during initial installation or routine cleaning, service and maintenance. Castors are an optional extra at additional cost, so you will need to tick this option.
  • Night blinds are soft curtains that can be drawn over the open-front display outside opening hours. Although not a standard feature, they are a great way to improve energy efficiency.
