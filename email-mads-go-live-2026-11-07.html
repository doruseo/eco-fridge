Why that date

  • The office is closed, so no orders are in flight. You trade Monday to Friday, 8.30 to 5.00. Switching on a Saturday morning means no customer is halfway through a basket and no order can fall between the two sites.
  • Quietest hours of the week. Early Saturday is the lowest traffic of the week, so the fewest people possible see any brief interruption.
  • A full weekend of buffer. Web address changes take a few hours to spread across the internet. Doing it on Saturday morning gives two clear days for that to settle and for us to watch the site, so you open on Monday with everything steady.
  • Not a Friday, not a holiday. We never switch a shop on a Friday, when a problem would sit unattended over the weekend.
  • It gives us the right amount of time. Nine days is enough for the remaining checks and the data copy without rushing, and not so long that the work goes stale.
